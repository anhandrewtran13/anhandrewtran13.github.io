01 / about

About Me

I'm a measurement-trained data scientist — psychometrics, clinical evaluation, causal inference — now focused on a simple question: is the model actually right, and how would we know?

At Kentro I'm a Senior Data Scientist and lead a team of 5 working on 10M+ clinical records from 15+ VA hospitals. We build LLM/NLP pipelines (keyword rules → transformer classifiers → LLM adjudication with expert review), predictive models for referral outcomes, and quasi-experimental evaluations of clinical decision-support tools. I own evaluation design for deployed models: holdout validation, calibration, label-quality audits, LLM-as-judge checks against human labels, and QA gates that stop bad releases.

Before that, I deployed a RAG chatbot and ran psychometric analytics for high-stakes medical certification exams at Inteleos, and spent six years researching patient outcomes on claims and EHR data at UC San Diego Health. On my own time I research chain-of-thought faithfulness and calibration in medical LLMs.

I also teach data analytics to undergraduates at the University of Maryland Global Campus and mentor early-career data professionals. My background bridges clinical psychology and computer science, so I care as much about the people behind the data as the models built on top of it.

Reasoning Faithfulness & Calibration in Medical LLMs

2026

Two-phase audit of medical reasoning models: does the stated chain of thought actually drive the answer (early-answer rate, biased-input flip rate, paraphrase sensitivity), and does the model know when it doesn't know (ECE, consistency, abstention)?
